1. Executive Context & Operational Challenges

Hospital administrators and financial controllers constantly struggle with balancing bed capacity, optimizing medication inventory, and minimizing insurance claim delays.

Core Challenge: Admission records, medical treatments, and billing summaries existed in isolated silos, preventing leadership from identifying cost leaks and operational bottlenecks across departments.

Solution: Developed a centralized, dynamic Power BI dashboard tracking patient admission velocity, Length of Stay (LOS), medication costs, and insurance claim distributions to drive data-informed clinical and administrative decisions.

2. Data Transformation & Analytical Framework

Stage 1 • Power Query

ETL & Cleaning

Cleaned patient demographic logs, standardized admission/discharge datetime types, and eliminated duplicate insurance provider codes.

Stage 2 • Data Modeling

Star Schema Setup

Structured Fact_Admissions linked directly to Dim_Patient, Dim_Doctor, Dim_Insurance, and Dim_Medication.

Stage 3 • DAX Engine

Complex KPI Formulation

Calculated dynamic measures for Average Length of Stay (ALOS), Net Billing per Department, and Admission Velocity across seasons.

3. 5-Module Clinical & Operational Breakdown

  • Module 1: Admission Overview: Monitors total patient intake trends, admission urgency types (Emergency vs. Elective), and seasonal surge patterns.
  • Module 2: Patient Demographics & Conditions: Identifies admission concentrations across age brackets, gender, and pre-existing medical conditions.
  • Module 3: Insurance & Billing Financials: Evaluates average billing amounts, insurance coverage ratios, and identifies high-cost treatment categories.
  • Module 4: Medication & Clinical Treatments: Tracks medication prescription frequency and associated pharmaceutical costs per admission.
  • Module 5: Operational Length of Stay (LOS): Benchmarks department-level bed occupancy and highlights extended stay anomalies.

4. Strategic Insights & Operational Recommendations

Analytical Finding Hospital Operational Context Strategic Recommendation
Length of Stay (LOS) Variance Certain chronic medical conditions exhibit an average LOS 40% higher than hospital benchmarks, causing bed shortages during admission surges. Discharge Planning Protocol: Implement automated milestone tracking for high-LOS conditions to streamline post-acute discharge coordination.
Insurance Claim Concentration Over 65% of billing volume is concentrated among top 3 insurance carriers, with recurring payment settlement delays in specific emergency treatments. Claims Workflow Automation: Pre-validate insurance eligibility during intake for elective admissions to accelerate claim settlement cycles.
Pharmaceutical Cost Concentration A narrow group of specialized medications accounts for a disproportionate share of total inpatient billing costs. Formulary Optimization: Partner with clinical heads to review generic substitution options without compromising clinical outcomes.
