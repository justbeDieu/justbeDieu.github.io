1. Project Overview & Business Objective

Olist connects thousands of small businesses across Brazil to direct marketplace consumers. Operating in a high-volume multi-category environment, leadership required a centralized reporting system to monitor overall commercial health and uncover operational bottlenecks.

Key Objectives:

  • Analyze overall sales growth, seasonal volatility, and average basket sizes (AOV).
  • Evaluate customer retention, purchasing habits by day of the week, and regional demand.
  • Identify high-impact product categories vs. underperforming inventory using volume-revenue benchmarking.
  • Assess seller concentration and fulfillment impact on customer review ratings.

2. End-to-End Analytics Workflow

To maintain data integrity and query efficiency, the project followed a structured 3-stage data lifecycle:

Stage 1 • SQL Layer

Data Prep & Views

Indexed key foreign keys; created Master View joining orders, payments, and reviews; established dedicated customer, product, and seller analytical views.

Stage 2 • Python Layer

Exploratory Data Analysis

Analyzed multi-year sales trends, calculated RFM customer segments, and investigated correlations between delivery delays and review drops.

Stage 3 • Power BI Layer

Interactive Reporting

Designed a 4-module dashboard featuring custom DAX KPIs, bubble charts for product segmentation, and state-level geographic heatmaps.

3. 4-Module Executive BI Architecture

  • Module 1: Sales Overview: Tracks Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), and Average Review Score alongside monthly growth trajectories and weekday purchasing trends.
  • Module 2: Customer Analysis: Examines total unique customer volume, repeat purchase behavior, and geographic customer concentration across Brazilian states (São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro).
  • Module 3: Product Performance: Utilizes a Revenue vs. Orders Bubble Chart to clearly separate high-margin niche items from fast-moving consumer products.
  • Module 4: Seller Performance: Benchmarks seller growth against customer demand, tracking top seller revenue contribution and fulfillment efficiency.

4. Key Insights & Actionable Recommendations

Analytical Finding Business Context Strategic Recommendation
Revenue Concentration (Pareto Principle) A small percentage of product categories (Health & Beauty, Watches, Bed & Bath) generate the vast majority of marketplace revenue. Cross-selling & Bundling: Prioritize top categories in marketing promotions and create combo discounts with slower-moving complementary accessories to raise overall basket size.
Weekday Purchasing Peaks Order volume consistently peaks from Monday to Thursday during working hours, with a marked decline over weekends. Campaign Scheduling: Allocate digital ad spend and launch flash-sale vouchers specifically on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons to capture peak user attention.
Parallel Supply-Demand Expansion Gross revenue growth correlates strongly with the simultaneous influx of both active buyers and new merchant onboardings. Regional Merchant Acquisition: Target seller onboarding in high-demand, underserved geographic states to lower interstate freight costs and delivery lead times.
Fulfillment Impact on Trust Overall customer ratings remain high; however, review scores drop sharply whenever delivery exceeds estimated delivery dates. Logistics SLA Enforcement: Establish merchant fulfillment SLAs (< 24h dispatch time) and offer fee rebates to high-performing sellers with zero delivery delay rates.
