Healthcare Analytics Power BI Dashboard

Power BI - Healthcare Analytics - Data Visualization - ETL - DAX - Data Modeling

PERSONAL PROJECT

Objective

Develop an end-to-end analytics solution to analyze a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace dataset and uncover insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product demand, and seller contribution.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, identify growth opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.

Implementation

  • Data Preparation: Consolidated and cleaned multiple datasets including orders, customers, products, sellers, and payments to create a structured analytical dataset.
  • Data Modeling: Designed a relational data model to support efficient analysis and reporting within Power BI.
  • Metric Development: Defined and calculated key performance indicators (KPIs) such as Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), Customer Growth, and Seller Performance.
  • Dashboard Development: Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with four analytical modules: Sales Overview, Customer Analysis, Product Performance, and Seller Performance, using charts, maps, and category-level comparisons.

Result

  • Delivered an interactive analytics dashboard providing a comprehensive view of marketplace performance across sales, customers, products, and sellers.
  • Identified key insights including revenue trends over time, high-performing product categories, geographic distribution of customers, and revenue concentration among top sellers.
  • The dashboard enables faster exploration of marketplace performance and helps identify strategic opportunities for category growth and seller optimization.
