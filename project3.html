1. Project Background & Business Challenge

Environmental monitoring agencies face challenges in managing massive, heterogeneous air quality datasets collected across hundreds of monitoring stations throughout the United States.

Core Challenge: Querying operational databases directly resulted in slow analytical performance and lacked the flexibility needed for multi-tiered drill-downs (from country level down to state, county, and seasonal timeframes).

Solution: Designed and deployed an end-to-end Enterprise Business Intelligence architecture: staging raw transactional logs, building SSIS ETL pipelines, structuring an OLAP multidimensional cube in SSAS, and delivering an executive Power BI reporting layer.

2. 4-Tier Data Warehouse Architecture

The solution implemented the complete Microsoft BI stack lifecycle to ensure high data consistency and zero report query lag:

1. Data Staging

Raw EPA datasets ingested and normalized in SQL Server staging area.

2. SSIS ETL

Automated cleaning, deduplication, surrogate key generation, and error logging.

3. SSAS OLAP

Pre-aggregated multidimensional cubes enabling sub-second drill-down queries.

4. Power BI

Executive dashboards with geographic heatmaps and parameter slicers.

Schema Design: Implemented a hybrid Star / Snowflake Schema centering on Fact_AQI_Measurement, connected to normalized Dim_Location (State ➔ County ➔ City), Dim_Date (Year ➔ Quarter ➔ Month ➔ Season), and Dim_Pollutant.

3. Key Environmental Insights & Policy Applications

Analytical Finding Environmental Context Actionable Recommendation
Seasonal Wildfire Spike (PM2.5) Western states (e.g., California, Washington) exhibit severe PM2.5 concentration spikes during Q3 dry seasons compared to baseline months. Targeted Public Health Alerts: Configure automated early-warning alert thresholds in local health monitoring systems ahead of late-summer wildfire seasons.
Urban Transportation Corridors (CO & NO₂) Metropolitan counties with high traffic density show elevated carbon monoxide levels consistently during morning and evening rush-hour intervals. Traffic Management Planning: Provide city planners with granular emission density maps to optimize public transit routing and green zone policies.
Sub-second Query Acceleration Pre-aggregating multi-year environmental observations into SSAS cubes reduced dashboard load times from seconds to sub-200ms. Scalable Architecture: The DW infrastructure is readily prepared to ingest new real-time IoT sensor telemetry without redesigning schemas.
