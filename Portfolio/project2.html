User Behavior Analysis for Ad Clicks

Python - Pandas - Scikit Learn - EDA - Data Visualization - Predictive Modeling - Google Colab

COURSE PROJECT - Team size: 4

Objective

To analyze user behavior in online advertising data and predict the likelihood of ad clicks, helping optimize marketing campaign strategies.

Implementation

  • Cleaned and preprocessed data by handling missing values and removing outliers.
  • Performed univariate and bivariate analysis to explore relationships between variables.
  • Visualized user behavior through distribution plots, correlation matrices, and heatmaps
  • Trained and evaluated multiple ML models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost) with cross-validation.
  • Measured accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score to assess performance.

Result

  • Achieved 85% accuracy with the Random Forest model.
  • Identified three major factors influencing click behavior: access time, device type, and campaign category.
  • Proposed data-driven improvements projected to increase CTR by ~15%.
